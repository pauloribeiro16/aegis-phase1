Proportionality: tier=MEDIUM. Third-party register covers 5-10
vendors with 2-3 critical ICT providers under DORA Art. 28
(typically cloud, payment processor, identity provider).

Gate criteria: tiered due diligence per risk; DPA + security schedule
for all critical providers; sub-processor change notification with
objection window; annual review of concentration risk; documented
exit strategy for each critical provider.

**Gate Status:** PASS (proportionate for MEDIUM under P2).
