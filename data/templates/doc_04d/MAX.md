## 7. Training Status

For MAX tier: continuous awareness programme with adaptive content;
annual certification per role; regulator-facing readiness drills;
mandatory DPO + CISO + CRO certification tracks; TIBER-EU
participation as attacking or defending entity.

## 9. Gaps and Known Limitations

- Audit Committee independence must be verified annually per BaFin
  MaRisk AT 9
- ICAAP-integrated ICT risk requires CRO + CISO joint sign-off on
  material risk assessments per DORA Art. 13
- Three-lines-of-defense must be operationalised with documented
  escalation paths between 1LoD (business), 2LoD (risk + compliance),
  and 3LoD (internal audit)
- Board subcommittees must meet minimum frequency per Terms of Reference
  (quarterly for Audit + Risk; semi-annual for Technology)
