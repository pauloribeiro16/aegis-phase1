# Maturity Consistency Template for MAX tier

## 6. Consistency Check (Maturity)

For MAX (systemically important) entities the maturity profile is
quantitatively managed:

- Current: 3 across all ten domains.
- Target: 4 uniformly. No domain may be below 4 because supervisory
  expectations (ECB SREP, BaFin MaRisk AT 9, DORA Art. 13, NIS2
  Art. 21) demand quantitatively managed controls.

This profile aligns with the role-models/MAX.yaml expectation:
Board + Audit Committee + Risk Committee + Technology Committee;
dedicated CISO + CRO + DPO + Head of Internal Audit; ICAAP-integrated
ICT risk; TIBER-EU tested controls; continuous regulatory engagement.

**Gate Status:** PASS — proportionate for MAX under P2.
Strict-mode note: any domain below target 4 must be accompanied by
a documented compensating control with a board-approved remediation
plan and explicit supervisory notification where required.
